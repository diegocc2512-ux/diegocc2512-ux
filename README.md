### 💧 Dynamic Process-Water Microbial & Disinfectant Model

Python-based dynamic model coupling **microbial kinetics, CSTR-style process-water turnover, contamination loading and disinfectant dynamics**.

The model was developed to investigate how process conditions influence microbiological control in industrial process-water systems and to support validation and optimisation strategies.

**Concepts:**

### 🌱 Second-Generation Bioethanol Biorefinery

Process-design project involving the retrofit of a Brazilian sugarcane biorefinery to convert **sugarcane bagasse and agricultural residues into second-generation bioethanol**.

The proposed process integrated:

**Biomass → Gasification → Syngas → Fermentation → Downstream Processing → Bioethanol**

My work included:

* Process-flow development
* Mass and energy calculations
* Equipment and operating-condition evaluation
* Process integration
* Economic analysis
* Sustainability assessment
* Waste valorisation
* Comparison of process alternatives

**Concepts:**
`Bioprocess Design` `Syngas Fermentation` `DSP` `Mass & Energy Balances` `Biorefineries` `Sustainability`
